## Platform

web

## Users

Primary: potential collaborators and partners (developers, ACG communities, sponsors) deciding whether VANT is a credible, interesting project to team up with. They arrive from a link (GitHub, Instagram, LinkedIn, Discord) and want to understand quickly what VANT is, what has actually been built, and who is behind it.

Secondary: anime / comics / games (ACG) fans curious about learning languages through the tools.

## Scope (updated 2026-10-09)

VANT is no longer only about language learning. It is an all-round creative project that accepts all kinds of creative work, including but not limited to: learning and AI tools (AnTiMa, TeaFlow, GAIA, where VANT started), photography (VANT Photography), common/practical projects such as survey apps and management apps, creative projects, and courses through VANT Academy (coming soon, in collaboration with practitioners). Survey and management apps are examples of work VANT takes on, not shipped products; do not present them as existing. Language learning through anime, comics and games is the origin story, not the whole identity.

## Product Purpose

VANT Project is a creative collective that grew out of Vibra and AnTiMa. It started with fun, AI-assisted ways to learn languages through Anime, Comics and Games, and now takes on any creative work (see Scope). The site is its home and portfolio. Success: a visitor explores the projects (case studies, screenshots, repos) and leaves convinced the work is real and worth collaborating on.

## Positioning

Language learning built from inside ACG culture rather than bolted onto it: a Discord bot living where fan communities already talk, an AI conversation tutor, and an AI text-RPG engine, all by one developer-led project. Vibra contributes the "simplify" philosophy (born from a 2023 game-jockey service, Vibra Store); AnTiMa (Anime Translation Manga) contributes the anime + language + AI direction. Vibra Store itself is a legacy project: VANT does not run or sell it, it only carries forward its meaning and objectives (remove friction, keep things simple). Never present Vibra Store as an active service.

## Operating Context

Visitors read on desktop and phone, often coming from Discord or social links. Indonesian and English audiences: the site is bilingual (EN / ID).

- Bilingual EN / ID content with a language switch.

## Product Principles

1. Show the built thing before describing the intention.
2. Fun is the method, not decoration: the ACG world is the point of entry.
3. Simplify: every interaction should feel effortless (Vibra's founding idea).
4. Honest scale: a small, developer-led project; never inflate it.
