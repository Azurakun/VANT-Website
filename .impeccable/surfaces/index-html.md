# Surface: VANT home (index.html)

Mode: Persuade. Audience: potential collaborators (devs, ACG communities, partners); secondary ACG fans. Action: explore the works, then reach out. Proof: real screenshots, public repos, live Discord. Constraints: static HTML/CSS/JS, EN/ID toggle, no invented metrics. User asked for a more professional feel than the previous catalog build, a dark black-and-white theme, intro animation, scroll-driven storytelling, a horizontal works gallery and micro-interactions.

## Direction contract

THESIS: VANT presented as a translated film: a letterboxed stage, and the story told in timed subtitle lines that resolve from Japanese into the reader's language. Refuses the neon AI-startup hero and the scroll-of-cards portfolio.

OWN-WORLD: near-black stage, off-white subtitle text, greys for secondary, hairline rules; monochrome, colour comes only from the real screenshots. Archivo (expanded caps for title cards, normal width for text), Noto Sans JP for Japanese lines, JetBrains Mono only for timecodes. Letterbox frames, timecodes, a scrub timeline, subtitle-track (CC) language switch.

STORY: intro title card; the stage shows the real works with a subtitle tagline; black-screen subtitles state the mission; Vibra and AnTiMa converge as two shots; the works play as a horizontal reel; credits roll for the developer; the closing scene asks to build together.

FIRST VIEWPORT: thin nav (wordmark, chapters, CC EN/ID). A wide letterboxed frame cross-fading project stills with timecode and scene label, VANT title card lower-left inside it. Beneath, a centered two-line subtitle (JP small, EN large) and two actions: Watch the works (primary), Join AnTiMa on Discord. Fixed scrub timeline at the bottom.

FORM: Subtitle Track, option A of the safer-register round, seed key ce11bde7. Signature interaction: subtitle lines show the Japanese caption with the EN/ID translation fading up beneath as they enter; the scrub timeline tracks and jumps chapters.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
